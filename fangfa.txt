Methods：

Sensitivity analysis. To assess the robustness of the projections to the choice of age-standardization weights, we repeated the BAPC analysis using three different sets of weights: (1) the age distribution of the study population (adolescents and young adults aged 10–24 years) derived from GBD population data (main analysis); (2) the WHO World Standard Population; and (3) equal weights across the three age groups (10–14, 15–19, 20–24 years). All other model specifications were held constant.

Results：

The projected trends were robust across all three weighting schemes (Supplementary Figure SX). At 2040, the projected age-standardized incidence rate ranged from 1520.9 to 1528.8 per 100,000 at the global level (maximum relative difference: 0.52%) and from X to X per 100,000 in China (maximum relative difference: 1.14%). Both the direction and magnitude of the projected decline were consistent across all scenarios.

Discussion：

The consistency of projections across different age-standardization weights supports the robustness of our findings and indicates that the projected decline is not an artifact of the choice of standard population.
